Support

Need a hand? Email hello@latticetech.ai from the address on your account and we'll get back to you. Please include what you were doing and, if you can, a screenshot.

Common questions

I didn't get my verification or password reset email

Check your spam or promotions folder, and make sure you used the same address you signed up with. You can request a new link from the sign-in page. Links expire after an hour.

How do I add my briefing to a podcast app?

In the web app, open the schedule you want, turn on audio, and copy its private feed link. In your podcast app, choose "add by URL" or "follow a show by URL" and paste it. Keep the link private: anyone with it can listen.

My runs stopped

You may have reached this month's usage cap. Your usage and the reset date are in Account settings. Runs resume automatically when the cap resets.

Results aren't relevant enough

Rate a few findings as relevant or not and say why. You can also edit the topic's brief, add sources you trust, or exclude ones you don't. Each run uses what you've told it.

I run My Morning News myself

For self-hosted installs, see the documentation on GitHub and open an issue there.
