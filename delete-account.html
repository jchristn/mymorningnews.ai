Delete your account

You can delete your My Morning News account and its data at any time.

From the web app

  1. Sign in at app.mymorningnews.ai.
  2. Open Account settings.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

From the mobile app

  1. Open the Account tab.
  2. Choose Delete account and confirm.

By email

If you can't sign in, email hello@latticetech.ai from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm by email.

What happens next

  • You're signed out everywhere right away, and scheduled runs, emails, and notifications stop.
  • After a 7-day grace period, your account and everything tied to it are permanently erased: topics, briefings, findings, audio, feedback, predictions, and settings. Signing in during the grace period cancels the deletion.
  • Usage records needed for cost accounting are kept without your name, email, or content.

Want a copy first? Use Export data in Account settings before you delete.
