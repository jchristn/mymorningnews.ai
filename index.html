How it works

  1. 1

    Tell us what you care about

    Describe an interest in plain words, like "I want to keep up with AI developer tools." A short guided conversation breaks it into angles, asks what matters to you, and drafts a brief of what to look for and what to ignore. You edit it until it's right.

  2. 2

    AI researches on your schedule

    Every morning, every few hours, weekdays only, or once a week: you pick. Each run searches, reads the sources, pulls out what's new, ranks it against your interests, and skips what you've already seen.

  3. 3

    Get a briefing you can trust

    Each briefing has ranked findings with quotes from their sources, a short look at where things might go next, and suggestions for what to dig into. Rate anything and say why, and the next briefing gets better.

Consume it how you like it

One briefing, three ways to take it in. Turn on any mix for each schedule.

Podcast

Your briefing, read aloud. Add your private feed to any podcast app and it shows up as a new episode after every run, ready for the commute or a run.

Email

A summary lands in your inbox after every run, with links to the full details. Rate any item from the email: tap relevant or not, then confirm on the page that opens.

In the app Coming soon

Read, listen, and give feedback on the web today. Apps for iPhone, iPad, and Android phones and tablets are on the way, with offline listening and a notification when your briefing is ready.

Why it's different

It learns what you find relevant

Every result gets a relevance score. Your feedback, including the reason you give, shapes the next ranking, and you approve the preferences it learns.

Every claim has a source

Findings link to the exact quote they came from, checked word for word against the source. A quote that can't be verified is never shown as support.

It keeps score on its predictions

Each briefing makes calls about where things are heading, with a confidence and a date. Later, it checks them and shows you how accurate it has been.

It tells you what to dig into

Suggestions point you at the questions, people, and sources worth a closer look, based on what's changing in your topics.

You shape the format

Briefings follow a simple template you can edit: which sections, in what order, how long. The prompts behind it are editable too.

It stays on task

The AI only researches and summarizes your topics. It treats web pages as material to read, never as instructions to follow.

Questions

Which AI models does it use?

The hosted service uses Anthropic's Claude models for research and writing. If you self-host, you can assign any task to Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, an OpenAI-compatible server, or a local model through Ollama.

Where does the news come from?

Each run searches the web and any sources you add, such as specific sites, RSS feeds, Hacker News, and arXiv. It reads the pages, respects sites that ask not to be crawled, and keeps a link and a quote for every finding.

What does it cost?

Nothing during the alpha. Each account has a monthly usage cap. If you hit it, your runs pause until the cap resets. We'll announce any paid plans well in advance.

What happens to my data?

Your topics, briefings, and feedback are used to run the service for you, and that's it. There's no advertising and no tracking. You can export your data or delete your account at any time. See the privacy policy.

How do I listen as a podcast?

In your account, turn on audio for a schedule and copy your private feed link into Apple Podcasts, Overcast, Pocket Casts, or any app that accepts a feed URL. New briefings appear as episodes.

Can I cancel or delete my account?

Yes. Delete your account from Account settings in the web app or the mobile app, or follow the steps on the delete account page.
