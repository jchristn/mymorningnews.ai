Privacy policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

My Morning News is operated by Lattice AI LLC ("we", "us"). It is in alpha. This policy describes how the hosted service at app.mymorningnews.ai handles your information. If you run My Morning News on your own server, your data stays with you and this policy does not apply.

The short version

  • We collect what we need to research your topics and deliver your briefings, and nothing more.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.
  • You can export your data or delete your account at any time.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, display name, time zone, language, and a hashed password. We never store your password in readable form.
  • What you ask us to follow: your topics, the answers you give the topic wizard, schedules, briefing formats, and preferences.
  • What we produce for you: briefings, findings, the source links and quotes behind them, predictions, suggestions, and audio files.
  • Your feedback: ratings and the reasons you give, which we use to rank future results for you.
  • Subscriptions and billing: your selected plan, signup offer, payment-provider account and subscription identifiers, purchase status, and generated audio usage. Card details are handled by the payment provider and are not stored by My Morning News.
  • Private provider connections: API credentials you supply are encrypted on the server and used to call the providers you select. You can remove these connections in Account settings.
  • Usage records: for every AI request made on your behalf, we record the task, the number of words processed (tokens), timing, and estimated cost. We use these to enforce usage caps and understand what the service costs to run.
  • Technical logs: requests to our servers, including IP address, browser or app version, and timing, kept for troubleshooting and security for a limited period.
  • Mobile app: if you turn on notifications, a push token for your device. The app stores your sign-in securely on your device.

How we use it

To run the service for you: research your topics, write and narrate your briefings, send your emails and notifications, rank results using your feedback, keep your account secure, enforce usage caps, and fix problems. We do not use your topics or feedback to advertise to you.

Services that process data for us

  • AI model provider (Anthropic): your topic descriptions, preferences, and the source material we gather are sent to Anthropic's API to research and write your briefings. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API data is not used to train their models.
  • Providers you connect: when you choose your own model or speech provider, the relevant research, briefing text and API authentication are sent to that provider under its terms.
  • Payments (Stripe, Apple and Google): the provider you purchase through processes your payment. We receive subscription verification and lifecycle information to manage access. Web checkout shares your account email and an account identifier with Stripe.
  • Email delivery (Mailgun): your email address and the content of the emails we send you.
  • Push notifications (Expo, Apple, and Google): your device's push token and short notification text, if you enable notifications.
  • Hosting: the servers that store the service's data.

Web pages we read to research your topics are fetched by our own servers. We follow sites' robots.txt rules.

Cookies and this website

This website sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts. It remembers your light or dark theme choice in your browser's local storage. The web app stores your sign-in in your browser so you stay signed in.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is active. Technical logs and the full text of AI requests are deleted on a rolling basis. Usage records without content are kept for cost accounting. When you delete your account, your application content and stored private provider keys are permanently erased after a 7-day grace period, during which signing in cancels the deletion. Payment providers retain their own transaction records under their retention policies. Signing back in does not restart a canceled subscription.

Your choices and rights

  • Export: download your topics, briefings, findings, feedback, and predictions from Account settings.
  • Correct: change your details in Account settings.
  • Delete: see how to delete your account.
  • Notifications and emails: turn any channel on or off per schedule.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your data. Contact us to exercise them.

Security

Connections are encrypted. Passwords are hashed, stored credentials are encrypted, access is limited by role, and sensitive actions are logged.

Children

My Morning News is for adults 18 and over. It is not intended for anyone younger, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and let account holders know by email.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@latticetech.ai.
